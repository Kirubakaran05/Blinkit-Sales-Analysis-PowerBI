An interactive, end-to-end Power BI dashboard designed to deliver comprehensive insights into Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution patterns across various outlet metrics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Objective

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into actionable business intelligence. By leveraging data modeling, DAX measures, and visual analytics, this dashboard helps stakeholders track key business performance indicators, analyze consumer preferences, and optimize operational strategies.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **💰 Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated across all outlets.
* **📊 Average Sales:** Average revenue generated per transaction.
* **📦 Items Sold:** Total volume of products sold.
* **⭐ Average Rating:** Average customer satisfaction rating across orders.

## 🛠️ Project Methodology & Workflow

1. **Requirement Gathering:** Collaborated to define core business metrics and align dashboard deliverables with stakeholder goals.
2. **Data Collection & Connection:** Connected raw Excel datasets directly into Power BI Desktop.
3. **Data Cleaning & ETL:** Cleaned, formatted, and handled missing values using **Power Query** to ensure data integrity.
4. **Data Modeling:** Established optimized relationships between tables to support dynamic filter propagation.
5. **DAX Calculations:** Engineered custom DAX measures for core KPIs, dynamic aggregations, and performance comparisons.
6. **Dashboard Layout & Design:** Built an intuitive UI utilizing a balanced palette with visual charts (Donut Charts, Bar Charts, KPI Cards, and Slicers).
7. **Testing & QA:** Validated formula outputs, interactive slicer dependencies, and dynamic filtering.
8. **Insights Generation:** Derived actionable business conclusions based on spatial, category, and outlet-level trends.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

* **Consumer Preferences:** Item type and fat content analysis highlight top-performing grocery categories to optimize marketing strategies.
* **Sales Performance:** Pinpointed highest-revenue product lines alongside underperforming categories for inventory optimization.
* **Geographic Trends:** Identified sales variations across outlet locations (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 cities) to support targeted expansion.
* **Outlet Management:** Evaluated how outlet size (Small, Medium, High) and establishment age impact sales output and operational efficiency.
